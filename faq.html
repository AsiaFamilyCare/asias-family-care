Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful Information for Families

Learn more about our childcare services, availability, scheduling, safety, and booking process.

What ages do you care for?

We provide care for children of all ages, from infants to teenagers. Care is tailored to each child’s age, routine, personality, and individual needs.

Do you provide care in my home?

Yes. We provide in-home babysitting and nanny services so your children can remain comfortable in their familiar surroundings.

Are you available 24 hours a day?

Availability may include daytime, evening, overnight, weekend, holiday, and last-minute care. All services are based on caregiver availability.

Do you offer overnight childcare?

Yes. Overnight care may be available for parents who work overnight, travel, attend an event, or need additional support.

Do you offer last-minute babysitting?

We do our best to assist with last-minute requests. Booking early is recommended, but families may still contact us for urgent care needs.

How far in advance should I book?

Booking as early as possible gives us the best chance of matching your family with an available caregiver. Short-notice requests are still welcome.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing varies based on the number of children, length of care, schedule, location, and type of service requested. Complete our request form to receive more information.

Can I request recurring childcare?

Yes. Families may request recurring, part-time, occasional, or one-time childcare. Scheduling depends on caregiver availability.

What information should I provide before care begins?

Please provide emergency contacts, allergies, medications, routines, house rules, bedtime instructions, and any special information your caregiver should know.

Can caregivers prepare meals for my children?

Caregivers may assist with simple meals and snacks when requested. Families should provide instructions about allergies, dietary restrictions, and preferred foods.

Do you provide transportation?

Transportation arrangements must be discussed and approved before care begins. Availability may depend on the caregiver and the family’s specific request.

How do I request childcare?

Select the button below and complete the childcare request form. Provide as much information as possible so we can review your needs.
